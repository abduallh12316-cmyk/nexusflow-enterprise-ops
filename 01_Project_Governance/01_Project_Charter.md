# Project Charter — NFX-001

**Project:** NexusFlow — Enterprise Operations Management Platform  
**Organization:** NexusFlow Technologies (fictional)  
**Sponsor:** Maha Al-Qahtani  
**Project Manager:** Faisal Al-Harbi  
**Project Coordinator:** Noura Al-Otaibi  
**Start:** 1 September 2026 | **Planned finish:** 26 February 2027  
**Budget baseline:** SAR 480,000

## Business need
Internal requests and approvals are spread across disconnected channels, limiting traceability and slowing decisions. NexusFlow will centralize requests, approvals, project/task tracking, KPI dashboards, notifications, and audit history.

## Success criteria
- MVP target go-live: 12 February 2027, subject to approved change control.
- 100% of in-scope critical workflows pass UAT.
- Zero open Critical defects at go-live.
- Mandatory security findings closed or formally accepted before release.
- Forecast final cost within 5% of SAR 480,000 baseline unless a change is approved.
- Operations accepts support runbook and handover pack.

## In scope
Authentication and role-based access; request submission; approval workflow; task/project tracking; KPI dashboard; reporting API; audit logs; email notification drafts/integration seam; bilingual interface labels; CSV export.

## Out of scope
Native mobile apps, SMS notifications, ERP replacement, unapproved SSO work, and production integrations without security approval.

## Governance
Weekly delivery review chaired by the PM. Sponsor approval is required for major scope/budget changes. Product Owner accepts business deliverables; Security reviews release posture; Operations accepts handover.
